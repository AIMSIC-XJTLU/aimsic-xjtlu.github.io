Neuromorphic Devices & In-Memory Computing

We study how semiconductor devices can represent synaptic weights and how memory arrays could use those weights for neural computation. Our projects include NS-RAM arrays in standard CMOS and circuit models of synaptic thin-film transistors.

NS-RAM research Synaptic transistor model

Analog & Mixed-Signal IC Design

Our projects span data conversion, analog front-ends, timing circuits and power conversion. Current work includes a noise-shaping SAR ADC and a transimpedance amplifier.
