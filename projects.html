Current researchNeuromorphic devices

Synaptic and neural behaviours in a standard silicon transistor

We study programming and readout in standard CMOS transistors, building on the NS-RAM concept of Pazos et al. (2025).

Current researchData conversion

A 15.97-ENOB 16.4-μW PPD NS-SAR ADC with Multiplexed Single CDAC and SAR Logic

A noise-shaping SAR ADC shares one capacitive DAC and one SAR logic block between two phases. The figures in the title are simulation results.

Current researchAnalog front-ends

Low-Power, High-Gain, High-Linearity Transimpedance Amplifier

We are designing a current-to-voltage amplifier for high gain and linearity at low power.
