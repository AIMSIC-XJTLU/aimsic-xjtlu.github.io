ABOUT AIMS

Devices, circuits

We connect semiconductor device research with integrated circuit design for artificial intelligence.

Who we are

The Artificial-Intelligence and Mixed-Signal Integrated Circuits Group (AIMS) is led by Professor Chun Zhao at the Emerging Electronics and Energy Technologies Research Centre, Xi’an Jiaotong-Liverpool University.

Our research focuses on neuromorphic devices and in-memory computing, and on the design of analog and mixed-signal integrated circuits.

A device-to-system perspective

Neuromorphic chips draw on how neurons respond to incoming signals and how synapses give some connections more weight than others. In-memory computing addresses a related challenge in AI hardware. When a memory array performs calculations using the weights it stores, the chip can spend less energy moving data between memory and the processor.

Our work on neuro-synaptic random access memory (NS-RAM) brings these ideas together. We study how electrical pulses change the conductance of standard CMOS transistors and whether those changes can represent synaptic weights. Measurements and simulations guide the design of circuits to read these signals from an array.

Alongside this work, we investigate data converters, timing circuits, power management and digital signal-processing interfaces.
